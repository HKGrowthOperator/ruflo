## Confirmed geometry & symbolism (from reference — now anchored)
- **H + K** as the two letterforms, deep green with a **gold/cream beveled outline**.
- **Trunk** grows up the centre, between/through the H and K = HK operating core.
- **Roots** fan out wide below the letters = foundation, processes, stability.
- **Crown** = full organic leaf canopy above, greens with pale cream highlights = growth/life.
- Style: hand-carved wood relief / illustrative, warm and natural — **not** flat/minimal,
  **not** tech/cyber. Confirms D4 = organic-leaf crown is on-brand.

## Background direction (informs brief D2)
Render 1's **dark olive + warm backlight halo** reads premium and natural. Recommendation:
the reveal background should be **warm-dark natural**, NOT cool/tech/blue. The automation flow
pulse stays a restrained cool accent so it's visually distinct from the warm crown light.

## ⚠️ Animation-consistency risk (critical — for logo-consistency-guard)
The reference is **highly detailed** (individual leaves, fine roots, carved texture, gold bevels).
Generative video (Higgsfield) cannot reliably hold this much micro-detail identical across frames →
high risk of: morphing leaves, shifting root filaments, warping H/K, flickering bevels.

**Recommended path (needs your decision — see brief §15 D8):**
- **Option A (safer, recommended):** commission/produce a **simplified flat vector** of the logo
  (clean H+K, simplified trunk, simplified canopy + roots) as the **animation master**. Use the
  detailed render as the static **hero/end-frame key art** only.
- **Option B (higher risk):** animate from the detailed render directly — expect distortion,
  heavier guard rejections, and more retries; reserve for a very short, low-motion reveal.

## Reproduce / avoid
- Reproduce: H+K geometry, trunk-through-letters, root fan, organic canopy, gold bevel, warm palette.
- Avoid: the small chip/erosion near the H crossbar in render 1 (artifact, not brand);
  any cool/tech recolor; flattening the crown into a generic shape.
