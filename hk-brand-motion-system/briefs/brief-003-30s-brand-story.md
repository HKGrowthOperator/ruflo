# Brief 003 — 30s HK Brand Story

- **ID:** brief-003 · **Piece:** Brand Story (full narrative)
- **Status:** READY (working palette/vector) — official hex/vector swap later

## 1. Goal
The full arc: from scattered, manual, trapped-in-operations chaos → an integrated system (roots,
HK core, the four branches connected by AI-as-tool) → real growth, freedom, time back.

## 2. Core message
HK integrates real solutions so deeply that founders work **ON** the company, not trapped inside it.
**"Erst das Fundament, dann das Wachstum."**

## 3. Scene structure (30s)
```
S1 0–5s    Tension: scattered, cold, disordered fragments (abstract — NO literal people).
S2 5–9s    Order begins: fragments gather; roots form (analysis + processes).
S3 9–14s   Trunk: HK as operating core; H+K integrate, readable.
S4 14–18s  Branches: the four service areas activate.
S5 18–22s  Flow lines connect the branches (automation/AI as a tool) — stop before the crown.
S6 22–27s  Crown blooms: growth, life, freedom, family, nature.
S7 27–30s  Full lock-up + tagline «TAGLINE» (review via hk-brand-language). Calm premium hold.
```

## 4. Tension handling (S1) — brand-safe
Render tension **abstractly**: cold, desaturated, tangled lines/fragments drifting in disorder.
Do NOT depict people, offices, or literal "trapped founder" (off-brand + generative risk). The
contrast (cold chaos → warm ordered system) carries the story.

## 5. AI allowed / not
Allowed: cool flow lines connecting branches (S5), as a tool. Not: AI in/over crown, robots, AI
icons, cyberpunk, HUD, religious, fantasy. Crown stays organic (S6).

## 6. Style & motion
Cold→warm journey. Premium, restrained, cinematic. Chaos resolves smoothly into ordered organic
growth. Near-still camera with a slow push-in allowed. Upright final lock-up.
