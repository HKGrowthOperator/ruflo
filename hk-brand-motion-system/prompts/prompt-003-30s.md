## Per-Clip Prompts (each ≤4s)

### C1 — Tension / chaos (0–2.5s)
```
[master style] BUT cold and desaturated: scattered tangled lines and small fragments drifting in
disorder on a dark field, restless and fragmented, the feeling of scattered manual work; no logo,
no people, no text; abstract.
```
### C2 — Order begins (2.5–5s)
```
[master style] The cold scattered fragments slow and begin to gather toward a center, color warming
slightly, disorder resolving into intention; still abstract; no logo yet; no text.
```
### C3 — Roots form (5–9s)
```
[master style] Warm-tan roots form from the gathered fragments and ground into a wide calm fan;
foundation, analysis, stability; warm light arriving; no letters yet; no text.
```
### C4 — Trunk + H/K (9–14s)
```
[master style] A trunk rises from the roots; the letters H and K resolve cleanly and readably in
deep green with a gold-cream edge, upright and exact, integrated into the trunk; confident settle;
no distortion; no text.
```
### C5 — Branches activate (14–18s)
```
[master style] Four branches extend from the trunk, the four service areas activating in turn, each
brightening with a small leaf accent in a distinct natural green; balanced; no icons; no text.
```
### C6 — Flow connects (18–22s)
```
[master style] Thin cool (#79C0D0) flow lines travel along roots, trunk and branches, connecting
the four branches as a calm network — automation and AI as a quiet tool — and STOP before the
crown; elegant, restrained; no UI, no icons; no text.
```
### C7 — Crown blooms (22–27s)
```
[master style] A full organic leaf crown blooms above in natural greens with pale highlights, warm
natural light, alive and breathing — growth, freedom, life; NO flow lines or tech in the crown; no text.
```
### C8 — Lock-up + tagline (27–30s)
```
[master style] The complete logo settles into an upright, centered, brand-accurate lock-up; a small
clean serif tagline appears beneath; calm premium hold; no rotation, no drift.
```
